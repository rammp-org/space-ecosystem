## Where it is leaning

Leaning: run the solenoid from the drive's motion output, so any cut engages the brake without firmware. A hardware hold-up of about 300 to 500 ms lets the electrical stop happen first.

## Why

Grabbing a disk brake at full speed works against "gentle by default".
